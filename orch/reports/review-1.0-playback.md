1. [blocker] application/vlc-android/src/org/videolan/vlc/media/PlayerController.kt:305 + PlaylistManager.kt:1431 + DropoutRecovery.kt:361. What is wrong: on an EncounteredError, the actor runs `setPlaybackStopped()` (state STOPPED, progress 0/0) before the playlist's listener. So for the whole error-path dropout wait, `service.isPlaying` is false, even though DropoutRecovery is going to start playback again. Every "is it playing?" guard therefore does nothing:
   - the call / transient focus loss check (`VLCAudioFocusHelper.kt:169`, `wasPlaying = service.isPlaying` → no pause);
   - headphones unplugged (`PlaybackService.kt:309`);
   - the sleep timer's expiry (`SleepTimer.kt:141`, `!player.playing -> finish`, nothing stopped);
   - `droppedStream`'s `player.isPaused()`, which is never true here.
   Failure: a call, a timer expiring or headphones pulled during a Wi-Fi↔mobile reconnect, and the stream comes back by itself, over the call or on the loudspeaker, all night long. A stream that errors while paused also reopens playing. Also `if (!service.isPlaying) waiting = false` runs at once, so the foreground/Wi-Fi "recovering" hold ends while libVLC is still connecting. Fix: when the guard takes over, set one explicit state (PLAYING or PAUSED as it was, captured before the actor's reset: keep the pre-error state in PlayerController) and make `isPlaying` and the stopped checks see "pending reopen". (CONFIRMED)
2. [major] PlaylistManager.kt:1487-1488. What is wrong: `waitingForReopen()` uses `recovering`, which stays true after `reopenCurrent()` until `look()` sees the time 1 s past the reopen point. Failure: during the reconnect's buffering, or right after a seek back, the user (or a notification's focus loss) pauses. That pause is a real `engine.pause()`. `play()` then takes the dropout branch: the UI says playing, libVLC stays paused. About 20 s later the stall watcher "reconnects", and on the third try it gives up and stops. While that lasts, the foreground service and the Wi-Fi lock are held while paused. Fix: separate "a reopen is pending (not yet opened)" from "recovering" and use the first in play()/pause(). (CONFIRMED)
3. [major] StreamDropout.kt:66 with DropoutRecovery.kt:268-273. Failure: Icecast/Shoutcast or CDN streams that cut each connection after a fixed time (a max listen time, a token expiry, e.g. 60 min) stop for good at the second cut. Fix: also require the stream to be seekable (live streams are not), or a third repeat. (PLAUSIBLE: server behaviour)
4. [major] StreamWifiLock.kt:17. What is wrong: `WIFI_MODE_FULL_LOW_LATENCY` is only active with the screen on and the app in the foreground (an activity, not a foreground service). 5. [major] WhisperWorker.kt:96 + TimeLimit.kt:27. What is wrong: when `setForeground` is refused (an automatic charging+idle job, or any job started from the background on Android 12+), the job runs as a plain job, and JobScheduler stops it at about 10 min (STOP_REASON_TIMEOUT). `stopped()` only treats FOREGROUND_SERVICE_TIMEOUT as final, so the job is marked `paused` and WorkManager starts it again from scratch, with no end. Failure: a long CPU transcription burns 10 min at a time, over and over, and never finishes. Fix: when the foreground was refused, or on STOP_REASON_TIMEOUT / runAttemptCount ≥ N, fail with a clear reason ("open the app"), or wait for resume. (PLAUSIBLE: the code path is certain, the trigger depends on the device)
