1. [major] components/Sheets.kt:350-363 — `GlassBottomSheet` runs `progress.animateTo(0f)` on its first composition even when closed. That sets `isRunning`, which turns `showing` true, so a closed sheet registers an `OverlayLayer` (a full-window scrim with alpha 0 that still takes taps, `focus.requestFocus()`, and a hazeEffect when blur is on). On the Timed tier (a touch tablet, or a phone held sideways = Rail) the tween runs its full 200 ms with nothing changing. Fix: only animate when shown (`else if (progress.value > 0f) animateTo(0f)`), compute `showing` with `derivedStateOf`, and compose the sheet only while it is open or closing.
