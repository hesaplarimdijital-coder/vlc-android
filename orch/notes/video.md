# NOTES FOR THE NEXT STORY — after story/player-wheel-share (PR #56): the wheel over the chrome
- `PlayerMouse` (vlc-android) drives a `PlayerMouse.Target` (the activity's `ActivityMouse`, or a test's); the wheel is the player's only where `PlayerChrome.isOverControls(x, y)` (window pixels) is false. DesktopPlayerChrome answers with `controls.surfaces` (`ChromeSurfaces`, player/ChromeSurfaces.kt): views (the PanelFrame, registered by PanelHost) and Compose bounds (`Modifier.chromeSurface(surfaces)`, a Modifier.Node with onGloballyPositioned/onDetach). Registered: the desktop bars' content (after their padding, not the shade), the touch bars (via `pokes`), the cards column, the recording indicator. A new interactive surface over the video must add `chromeSurface(visibility.surfaces)` or the wheel there sets the volume.
- `Modifier.keepsWheel()` at PlayerPanel's root consumes what the tab's scroll leaves. Sheets/menus/dialogs are windows of their own (never reach the activity).
- Test: `PlayerWheelTest` builds the views like attach() does (ComposeView over a root FrameLayout + PanelHost) and sends ACTION_SCROLL MotionEvents (SOURCE_MOUSE) through PlayerMouse.handle, then itself to the Compose view (ViewRootForTest) under the point, location offset into that view. Do NOT send wheel events through activity/decor dispatchGenericMotionEvent on Robolectric: API 34's ViewGroup.dispatchGenericPointerEvent picks the child at (x, x) (reads getXDispatchLocation twice), and for SOURCE_MOUSE at the cursor position, which Robolectric's MotionEvent lacks (0, 0), so the turn lands in the wrong view. Compose itself positions by rawX/rawY minus the event's own window offset, so any consistent offset is fine.
